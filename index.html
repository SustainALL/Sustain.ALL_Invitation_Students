How to get involved

No sustainability background required — just curiosity and a willingness for transdisciplinary collaboration.

1

Application

Apply to the Sustain.ALL Transformation Hub as a student from the Berlin University Alliance — TU Berlin, FU Berlin, HU Berlin or Charité. Students from all disciplines are welcome. Apply with a short motivation letter to berispek@tu-berlin.de until 10/10/26

2

Challenge Matching

Choose the challenge according to your interest and join an interdisciplinary team passionate to solve the same challenge.

3

Build

Work directly with challenge partners, supported by weekly coaching and workshops. Move from research and problem definition toward a concept, prototype or pilot.

04

Showcase & Continue

Present your solution at the end-of-semester showcase. Your solution then serves as the foundation for a new team to work on for the next semester!

About the hub

Education for sustainable development, with your challenge at the centre

Sustain.ALL is an educational and transformation initiative at the Technische Universität Berlin that links Education for Sustainable Development (ESD) with real-world processes of change. Instead of case studies, you work directly on real challenges brought to us by university, industry and society partners.

Execution-based Teaching

Interdisciplinary student teams investigate a challenge throughout the semester, moving from research and problem definition to a co-created concept, prototype or pilot. The expected outcome depends on the complexity and starting point of each challenge.

University-wide Collaboration

Administration, faculties, students and researchers all feed challenges, data and expertise into the hub to achieve a working solution, collectively.

Bridge between different worlds

We sit between society, industry and academia, encouraging the transdisciplinary collaboration that climate resilience deserves.
