Semester Plan

No sustainability background required — just curiosity and a willingness for transdisciplinary collaboration.

1

Application

Apply to the Sustain.ALL Transformation Hub as a student from the Berlin University Alliance — TU Berlin, FU Berlin, HU Berlin or Charité. Students from all disciplines are welcome. Apply with a short motivation letter to berispek@tu-berlin.de until 10/10/26

2

Challenge Matching

Choose the challenge according to your interest and join an interdisciplinary team passionate to solve the same challenge.

3

Build

Work directly with challenge partners, supported by weekly coaching and workshops. Move from research and problem definition toward a concept, prototype or pilot.

04

Showcase & Continue

Present your solution at the end-of-semester showcase. Your solution then serves as the foundation for a new team to work on for the next semester!

Grading

If you help the world become more sustainable, you should get a 1.0!

That was our plan, but the TU disagreed:(
So here is the grading system that the TU agreed with. We have a Portfolio Exam with 4 deliverables that make up the entire grade.

Project Proposal

3 pages proposal describing a sustainability-focused project to be tackled during the semester (including project development plan). This makes up 20% of the grade.

Mid-term Presentation

Midterm Presentation (15 min) + Q&A (15 min). The presentation includes the status of on project development, challenges, and solutions. This makes up 30% of the grade.

Final Presentation

Final Presentation (15 min) + Q&A (15 min). The students present the final status of the sustainable solution developed in the practical project. This accounts for 30% of the grade.

Project Wiki or Podcast

An online Wiki-style project page documenting their process, methods, and outcomes, or a short podcast episode (10 minutes) presenting their project journey and key insights along with a 1-page individual reflective report. This accounts for 20%.
